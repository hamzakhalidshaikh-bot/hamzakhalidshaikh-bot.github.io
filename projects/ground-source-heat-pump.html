ACADEMIC RESEARCH

Ground Source Heat Pump Design and Performance Analysis

A building-energy modelling study exploring heating and cooling demand and geothermal heat exchanger sizing under different climatic conditions.

Academic projectNov 2025 – Nov 2026Thermal systems / simulation

01 / The engineering problem

How could a ground-source heat pump system be assessed for a large mixed-use building in climates with different heating and cooling demands? The work required consistent modelling of building loads before examining the ground loop.

Conceptual illustration created for the portfolio. It is not an original simulation output or facility drawing.

02 / What I worked on

I led a three-member project team, coordinated responsibilities, modelled building heating and cooling loads using HAP, and used GLHEPro to investigate ground heat exchanger sizing. The study included climate cases such as Karachi and Abbottabad.

03 / Approach and workflow

Define building modelOrganize the building into thermal zones and define the input assumptions needed for load modelling.
Estimate thermal demandUse HAP to study cooling and heating requirements and identify the main operating characteristics.
Investigate the ground exchangerApply loads in GLHEPro to examine borefield sizing and thermal performance.
Interpret feasibilityCompare outcomes across climatic conditions and document important model limitations.

05 / Engineering reflection

The central challenge was not simply selecting a heat pump. It was understanding whether the building load profile and the ground heat exchanger could work together over time. A well-documented portfolio should communicate that reasoning rather than only displaying software outputs.
